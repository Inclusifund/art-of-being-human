About me

My name is Doryan, and I have been on this path of service and self realization since my late teens.

At 17 years old, I had an ego death experience which led me to pursue a path of service, of going within myself.

The question

“How can I bring people back home, back to their heart?”

I first started studying human consciousness intentionally, walking the path of plants in private settings. I wanted to understand how deep we humans could go, and understand more the Divine Consciousness and Love that I had felt previously.

I then studied various practices and modalities until one day the path of plants opened up again.

It wasn't a personal choice per se. Life opened all avenues for me to walk this path effortlessly.

All came very naturally for me, as I know these ceremonies deeply, I know the way of the heart, and I know these other realms from my previous lives.

I do my best every day to walk the path of the heart, and to remind others they are the medicine.

The biggest gift we can give ourselves is to love deeply oneself.

In service

In the past years, I have been serving about 180+ people a year with various individual and group ceremonies.

I have travelled the world to do these, and have been meeting people from all over the world and from all walks of life.

It's been truly a blessing.

The invitation

I am grateful to be in my dharma, and on the path of service.

I hope to cross path with you, and support you in your remembering the best that I can.

Thank you for taking your precious time to read this section about me.

Walking each other home, remembering.
