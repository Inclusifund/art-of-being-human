Trish

To the tender hearts at the end of the world: you are so beautiful.

Who I am talking to

You are still here, despite all invitations to deaden your soul. You keep my heart beating strong.

I’m talking to you. The ones with brave, courageous hearts who are able to hold multiple coexisting truths at the same time.

The ones walking in multiple worlds,
holding onto the truth of their hearts,
bodies, and ancestral wisdom.

What we were given

From our moment of birth, we have been groomed within a countless number of familial, social, cultural, religious, educational, and institutional paradigms that are not reflective of our true nature.

Which is love.

About me

I’m Trish. I’m a metaphysical bridge-builder of Celtic ancestry walking a path of service to collective social healing and planetary liberation.

On this earthwalk I find expression as a frequency worker, ceremonialist, alchemist, international educator, edgewalker, spiritual activist, minister, author, podcaster, and former professor.

I embrace the both/and of life on this earth plane, in service to the great Cosmic Mother.

Holding the space

A seasoned facilitator and spaceholder, I serve as a safe anchor for people to tap into vulnerable places in themselves that are in need of love and deeper healing, so they can come into full acceptance of all aspects of themselves.

Often our so-called shadow traits are simply the parts of ourselves that we have disowned, and are in need of unconditional love, tenderness, and understanding.

Keeper of the Flame

One of my roles is to gently guide you back into your heart — and to prepare your nervous system for you to feel safe enough to do so.

So you can tap back into your multidimensional nature, and remember why you came to this earth plane at this time.

I am passionate about working with changemakers, visionaries, and New Earth leaders to heal from intergenerational trauma, so that they have full access to the gifts of their soul blueprint in this life expression.

The edgewalker

As an edgewalker, my life is a walking paradox, a moving sea of contradictions. Always, I lead with curiosity.

I am a between worlds walker in most every sense. My body moves across borders and cultures, and I find myself welcomed warm-heartedly by people of vastly different colors, creeds, and ancestries.

This is what happens when we begin to step out of hiding and into our authentic self.

The unlearning

We often move through our lives completely unaware of the degree to which unconscious programming is running the show, structuring our consciousness. So many of the behavioral patterns and belief systems that frame our reality are not actually “ours” at all.

Unlearning who we thought we were, to come home to a more nurturing, nourishing, authentic state of existence, takes courage, curiosity, and a whole lot of compassion for ourselves.

As we walk a path of love, we are invited again and again to release any beliefs, behaviors, and patterns that insult our soul. To let only love remain.

Let our coming together be living ceremony,
activated collective prayer.
Let us be the light in the dark for each other.
Let us stay tender for each other.

May we all remember ourselves home.
